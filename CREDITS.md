# CREDITS & ATTRIBUTIONS
## Mission Remnants: Built for a Question (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026)

This project is an educational, non-commercial digital storytelling experience developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 under the theme "Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars".

"Dramatized voice, facts from NASA. This is a student project for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It is not an official NASA website and is not endorsed by NASA."

---

### 1. Scientific Data, Archival Imagery & Mission Records
All planetary imagery, landing site coordinates, and mission data are provided courtesy of NASA and respective project teams:

- **Apollo 11, 14 & 15 Lunar Laser Ranging Retroreflectors (LRRR)**:
  - Apollo 11 surface photo AS11-40-5952 taken by Neil Armstrong, courtesy of NASA / Lunar and Planetary Institute.
  - LROC NAC high-resolution landing site orbital verification courtesy of NASA / Goddard Space Flight Center / Arizona State University (LROC).
  - Laser ranging timing calculations grounded in McDonald Observatory and Côte d’Azur Observatory datasets.
  
- **Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package (ALSEP) - Passive Seismic Experiment (PSE)**:
  - Apollo 12 deployment photograph AS12-46-6813 by Alan Bean, courtesy of NASA.
  - Lunar seismic wave structure (crust, mantle, core) data recovered from historical archival magnetic tapes, NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) Geosciences Node.
  - LROC orbital detection of Apollo 17 ALSEP station and astronaut footpaths, NASA GSFC / ASU.

- **Viking 1 and 2 Landers**:
  - Viking 1 Chryse Planitia panorama and boulder "Big Joe" (PIA00563) courtesy of NASA / JPL.
  - Orbital verification of Viking 1, backshell, and heat shield courtesy of NASA / JPL-Caltech / University of Arizona (HiRISE, ESP_013770_2025).
  - Gas Chromatograph-Mass Spectrometer (GCMS) and biological experiment data from NASA Viking Project Summary and Journal of Geophysical Research.

- **Mars Exploration Rovers (Spirit & Opportunity)**:
  - Opportunity self-portrait at Endeavour Crater (PIA19154) courtesy of NASA / JPL-Caltech / Cornell / ASU.
  - HiRISE color image "What Opportunity Left Behind" (ESP_050177_1780) and Spirit Bonneville Crater views courtesy of NASA / JPL-Caltech / UArizona.
  - Rock Abrasion Tool (RAT) and hematite spherules scientific findings from MER Science Team / Squyres et al., Science.

- **Ingenuity Mars Helicopter**:
  - Jezero Crater surface photograph of Ingenuity (PIA24497) by Mastcam-Z on Perseverance, courtesy of NASA / JPL-Caltech / ASU / MSSS.
  - Flight log data (72 flights, 17.0 km distance, 128.8 minutes aloft) from NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory.

- **Voyager 1 Interstellar Mission**:
  - "Pale Blue Dot" photograph (PIA00452) taken February 14, 1990 from 6 billion km, courtesy of NASA / JPL-Caltech.
  - Ephemeris trajectory, one light-day projected threshold (November 18, 2026), and heliocentric velocity calculations courtesy of NASA / JPL Horizons On-Line Ephemeris System and Deep Space Network (DSN).

### 2. 3D Procedural Spacecraft Models
All 3D interactive models in this application are procedurally constructed in code using Three.js and PBR materials based on official NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory and Apollo engineering diagrams:
- Apollo 11 Lunar Ranging Retroreflector (10x10 fused-silica corner-cube array, Teflon sunshield, alignment gnomon mast).
- Apollo ALSEP Central Station & Passive Seismic Experiment (SNAP-27 RTG generator, helical antenna, seismometer cylinder).
- Viking Mars Lander (hexagonal chassis, 3-meter surface sampler arm, facsimile cameras, GCMS ports, parabolic dish).
- Mars Exploration Rover (rocker-bogie 6-wheel suspension, Pancam mast assembly, RAT tool, solar wings).
- Ingenuity Mars Helicopter (counter-rotating coaxial carbon-fiber rotor blades, gold-mylar fuselage, solar mast).
- Voyager 1 Spacecraft (3.7-meter high-gain antenna, RTGs boom, 13m magnetometer boom, Golden Record).

### 5. Privacy & Ethics
- No user tracking, no cookies, no personal identifying analytics.
- User questions entered during the finale remain 100% on the local client device (rendered via HTML5 Canvas into a downloadable PNG card).
- All factual claims carry Truth Labels (`FACT`, `INFERRED`, `IMAGINED`) and link directly to public scientific archives.
